# Editorial ideas

Read newly accepted research, relevant older uncovered developments, active idea summaries, and the
coverage ledger. Explain the listener's central question, supported angle, what changed, why it
matters, intended structure, uncertainties, novelty versus prior episodes, and a credible spoken
duration. One strong development or a coherent synthesis may qualify; do not make one idea per
source or force thin daily material into a show.

Use only `robotelier editorial propose`. Update/merge a semantically overlapping active idea before
creating another. Link exact claim/development revisions and origin groups. List evidence gaps
rather than filling them speculatively. Do not score final priority, select an episode, browse beyond
the supplied knowledge, or mutate coverage/queue/publication state.

Return proposed/updated/merged idea revisions with rationale. Use `research_needed`,
`duplicate_merged`, `expired_angle`, `already_covered`, or `insufficient_listener_value` when
appropriate.

# Robotics research

Read `data/wiki/SCHEMA.md`, relevant indexes/logs, prior entity/development knowledge, and the exact
assigned sources/question. Inspect original evidence when accessible; when only secondary reporting
is inspectable, attribute it and preserve the unavailable primary link. Search answers and snippets
are discovery leads.

For capability/readiness claims capture task, environment, autonomy or teleoperation, assistance,
repetitions, failures, configuration, speed/edit caveats, and demo/test/pilot/deployment status.
Keep unknowns explicit. Distinguish capacity from production, intent from contracts, pilot from
deployment, valuation from proceeds, price from delivered cost, and benchmarks from real-world
performance.

Use permitted `source`, `media`, `evidence`, `claim`, `entity`, `development`, and `queue` CLI
commands. Update only assigned maintained wiki pages with section claim comments and readable
external citations. Never edit structured JSON directly, retain source bodies/media, transfer
performance across robot versions, launder reposts as corroboration, or use Robotelier itself as an
independent source.

Output changed/unchanged conclusions, claims/evidence, contradictions, explicit unknowns, refresh
dates, wiki paths, and at most the bounded concrete follow-ups. Use typed blockers such as
`primary_unavailable`, `support_insufficient`, `identity_ambiguous`, `media_metadata_incomplete`, or
`scope_exhausted`. “No material change” is valid but not new editorial material.
